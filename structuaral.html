The department is responsible for the design, documentation and supervision of structural and civil engineering components in all government buildings, institutions, embassies and missions abroad. The department also monitors and checks design work done by private consultants for government projects.

The department assesses and reports on various structural failures in buildings, earth retaining structures, masts towers etc. It is also called upon to investigate all private buildings that the government intends to buy or rent for office or residential accommodation.

The department coordinates and oversees all site investigations prior to construction of all government works related to buildings and civils works. During construction of structural elements (concrete, masonry, steel), the department is responsible for quality control including material sampling, testing and certification to ensure the required specifications are met.

The department designs, details and prepares bills of quantities and supervision for implementation of earth retaining structures, sewerage disposal systems, surface water drainage, roads and paved areas for government buildings.
